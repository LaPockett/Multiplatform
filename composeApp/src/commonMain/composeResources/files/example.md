# Our offer of bags is unbeatable

At the beginning of the 2024, we begin acquiring more popular and versatile pieces, those that tend to attract a broader audience. However, we quickly noticed that they did not meet the expectations of our ideal clients, since they usually have these pieces already in their closet. Therefore, we redefined our purchasing strategy to focus on the extraordinary.

> At LOGO, we position ourselves as the perfect extension of our members' closets, who already have INCREDIBLE closets. Our challenge  during this MVP start has been to live up to those expectations. Someone told us "Fake it till you make it" 😇

- **Rare and difficult to find pieces**, such as the iconic Bottega Veneta Foulard, sold out worldwide.
- **Unexpected colors** that challenge the norm, like the Mini Lady Dior in an exclusive Baby Blue tone.
- **Unique and "conversation" models**, such as Jonathan Anderson's controversial Pigeon Clutch.
- **Emerging trends** that our customers want to try before committing, like the Gucci Jackie Ancora.

This strategy not only captured the attention of our ideal members, but also significantly boosted their retention, creating what we could call a true ****"LOGO addiction."**** These members constantly come back with a recurring question: “What's new?” Furthermore, we have observed that price is no longer a concern for them; If they like something, they are willing to pay what is necessary to enjoy it.

> As they enjoy the LOGO experience, many of our members stopped using their own wardrobe, contacting us to explore the possibility of renting their pieces to third parties through our service. This behavior allows us to expand our offer and complement the LOGO collection with pieces that also attract an audience that is starting out in the world of luxury and looking for more common and popular pieces

## LOGO's closet remains in constant motion.

Always bringing in bags that we know will work and selling out the pieces that we see are not working at the pace we set. The process of adding third-party bags to LOGO is very straight forward. We carry out a series of authenticity checks, even using external applications if necessary. However, the vast majority of verifications are done thanks to our expertise in the luxury sector. If everything is verified, we proceed to sign the temporary responsibility contract and send our LOGO Box, in a convenient flat format, to the owner. The owner then places the bag inside our LOGO Box, which is very easy to assemble (see video), and hands it back to the courier, who returns it to LOGO.

When we receive the bag at LOGO, we verify that it matches the model and that its condition is the same as described in the contract. If it shows significant wear or damage, we proceed with repairs, always with the owner’s prior approval of the repair costs. If the bag is in good condition, LOGO carries out a bag pampering, including a deep cleaning/sanitizing to remove odors and signs of use, this service is covered by us.

Once the bag reaches its final member, we proceed with the payment to the owner. The amount and LOGO’s profit percentage have changed over time as we have conducted rentals, trials, and cost analyses. Initially, we kept only 30% to be more attractive to owners, and it worked. Currently, the percentage is around 50%, corresponding to our intermediation commission. Moving forward, our commission structure will continue to evolve based on inventory diversity, model desirability, rental probability, and the item’s appraisal or second hand market value.
